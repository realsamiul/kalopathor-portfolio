Executive Summary

Demand Intelligence

HYPERION LPG Demand Forecasting converts monthly national consumption data into accurate, seasonality‑aware forecasts using XGBoost, capturing long‑term upward trend and predictable annual cycles for planning and inventory optimization.

The pipeline standardizes inputs, engineers temporal/seasonal features, validates via time‑series CV and holdout evaluation, and outputs monthly demand with confidence bands for production and logistics scheduling.

Methodology

Pipeline Architecture

Preprocessing: Normalize, align, and validate monthly series; apply outlier detection and imputation rules only under tight thresholds.

Feature engineering: Concatenate trend, seasonal, and lagged terms to expose multi‑scale dynamics to tree‑based learners.

Modeling: XGBoost trained with time‑series CV and final evaluation on a recent holdout to confirm generalization prior to deployment.

Core Mathematics

Key Formulas

Key formulas guiding the forecasting system:

Fourier Seasonality

Annual seasonal components:

$$ s_{k}(t) = a_{k}\sin\left(\frac{2\pi kt}{12}\right) + b_{k}\cos\left(\frac{2\pi kt}{12}\right) $$

MAPE

$$ \text{MAPE} = \frac{100}{N}\sum_{t=1}^{N}\left|\frac{y_{t}-\hat{y}_{t}}{y_{t}}\right| $$

As a primary interpretability metric

RMSE

For absolute error benchmarking:

$$ \text{RMSE} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N}\sum_{t=1}^{N}(y_{t}-\hat{y}_{t})^{2}} $$

Implementation

Code Snippets

Feature Construction

t = np.arange(len(y))
month = pd.Series(index=y.index, data=y.index.month)
# Fourier seasonality
k = 2
S = []
for j in range(1, k+1):
    S.append(np.sin(2*np.pi*j*t/12))
    S.append(np.cos(2*np.pi*j*t/12))
fourier = np.vstack(S).T
# Lags
X = pd.DataFrame({"t": t, "month": month})
for L in [1,3,6,12]:
    X[f"y_lag_{L}"] = y.shift(L)
X = X.join(pd.DataFrame(fourier, index=y.index).add_prefix("f"))
X_train, y_train = X.dropna(), y.loc[X.dropna().index]
model = xgb.XGBRegressor(max_depth=6, n_estimators=200, learning_rate=0.05)
model.fit(X_train, y_train)
